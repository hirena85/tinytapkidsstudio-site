Stick City Z privacy policy

App: Stick City Z (Android, package com.tinytapkids.stickcityz)
Developer: Tiny Tap Kids Studio
Website: https://tinytapkidsstudio.com
Contact: hrr4.h.a@gmail.com
Effective date: November 15, 2026

Short version: Stick City Z is a free game for a general audience (13+) that shows ads from Google AdMob. We (Tiny Tap Kids Studio) do not run any servers and do not collect, store or sell your personal information ourselves. Your game progress stays on your device. Google processes some data to show ads, as described below.

1. Who we are

Stick City Z ("the app", "the game") is made by Tiny Tap Kids Studio ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at hrr4.h.a@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your device, in the app’s local storage: your coins, the heroes you have unlocked and your chosen hero, the date each hero’s daily ad try was last used, your weapons, weapon and glider upgrades and other upgrades, the levels you have cleared, your best scores (such as kills, survival time, combo, climb, rooftop chain, giants felled and best Endless wave), daily run progress, your last run summary and your settings (camera distance, vibration and button size). This data is never sent to us or to any server we operate.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Android's own device backup may include this data in your Google account backup if you have device backup turned on. That backup is controlled by you and Google.

3. No accounts

The game has no user accounts, no online leaderboards, no chat and no user-generated content. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information. The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own and has no in-app purchases. Heroes, weapons and upgrades are unlocked with coins earned by playing.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a full-screen ad from Google AdMob at the end of a run (at the game-over screen, or when you leave a run for the menu), never in the middle of the action. It also offers optional rewarded ads, which you can choose to watch to revive once in a run, to double the coins from a run, to open a special weapon crate, or to try a locked hero for one run (once per hero per day). Ads are limited to a “PG” content rating. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is derived
  • information about the device and app, such as model, operating system version, app version, and launch and performance diagnostics
  • your interactions with ads, such as views and taps

This data is encrypted in transit. Google may use it to show personalized ads where you have consented (or where consent is not required), as well as for ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

5. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent or show your privacy choices before ads are personalized. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads. To change your choice later, contact us, or clear the app's data in Android settings so the consent message is shown again the next time you open the game.

6. Permissions

The app uses internet and network-state access (to load ads) and vibration (for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's settings). The Google Mobile Ads SDK and standard Android libraries also add the advertising ID permission and a few technical permissions (such as wake lock and basic phone state) automatically. The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.

7. Children

The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It contains cartoon zombie action. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention and your rights

We do not hold any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to retain, export or delete. Data processed by Google for ads is handled under Google's policies linked above, and you can manage it through your Google account and your device settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools, and contact us with any questions.

9. Security

All communication between the app and Google's ad and consent services uses encryption (HTTPS/TLS). The game does not transmit your data to us, or to any party other than Google.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example because a new feature needs it, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

11. Contact
