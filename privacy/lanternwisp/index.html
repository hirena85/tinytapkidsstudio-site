Lanternwisp privacy policy

App: Lanternwisp: The Ember Vale (Android, package com.tinytapkids.lanternfall)

Developer: Tiny Tap Kids Studio

Website: https://tinytapkidsstudio.com

Contact: hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Short version: Lanternwisp is a free story action RPG for a general audience (13+), and the whole story is free. It offers optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob that you can choose to watch for an in-game bonus. It never shows an ad you did not ask for. It also has local co-op with friends on the same Wi-Fi. We (Tiny Tap Kids Studio) do not run any servers and do not collect, store or sell your personal information ourselves. Your progress stays on your device. Google processes some data for ads, as described below.

1. Who we are

Lanternwisp: The Ember Vale ("the app", "the game") is made by Tiny Tap Kids Studio ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your device, in the app's local storage: your hero's level, gear, items, quests, story progress, Wisps, your bramblehorn Burr, collections and journal. It also saves your settings (sound, music, camera, text speed, story intro) and the name you choose for co-op, and it keeps simple counters of how many optional ads you have watched today, so it can limit them. This data is never sent to us or to any server we operate.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Android's own device backup may include this data in your Google account backup if you have device backup turned on. That backup is controlled by you and Google.

3. No accounts, chat or tracking of our own

The game has no user accounts, no chat and no user-generated content. We never ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information. The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

4. Local co-op on the same Wi-Fi

Up to four phones on the same Wi-Fi network can play together. The phones find each other with a small message on your local network and then connect directly to each other. During a co-op session, the phones in that session exchange what they need to play together: the co-op name each player chose, each hero's appearance and position, and the shared game state, such as enemies, quests and world changes. This data stays on your local network. It is not sent to us or to any internet server, and it stops when you leave the session. Co-op is optional; the game asks Android for network access so that it can work.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob), opt-in only

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch a short ad for a bonus, for example to get back up where you fell, to double a bounty reward or to get a treat for Burr. The game never shows banner ads or full-screen ads you did not ask for. It never offers ads during the story or in combat, and it limits how often ads can be offered. Ads are limited to a "PG" content rating. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is derived
  • information about the device and app, such as model, operating system version, app version, and launch and performance diagnostics
  • your interactions with ads, such as views and taps

This data is encrypted in transit. Google uses it for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

6. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are personalized. It asks on the title screen, after the story intro. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy options (ads)" button in the game's Settings, which is shown where this option is required. If you do not consent and you choose to watch an ad, you may still see a non-personalized or limited ad.

7. Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases at this time. If we add an optional purchase in the future, Google Play will process it, and we will update this policy before it launches.

8. Children

The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and your rights

We do not hold any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to retain, export or delete. Google handles the data it processes for ads under its policies linked above, and you can manage it through your Google account and your device settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. Contact us with any questions.

10. Security

Communication between the app and Google's ad and consent services is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Co-op traffic stays on your local network, between the phones in the session. The game does not send your data to us.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example because a new feature needs it, we will update this page and its effective date. We will also mention significant changes in the app's release notes.

12. Contact
