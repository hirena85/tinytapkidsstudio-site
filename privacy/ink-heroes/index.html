Ink Heroes privacy policy

App: Ink Heroes (Android package and iOS bundle ID com.tinytapkids.inkheroes, for Android phones and tablets, iPhone and iPad)
Developer: Tiny Tap Kids Studio
Website: https://tinytapkidsstudio.com
Contact: hrr4.h.a@gmail.com
Effective date: November 16, 2026

Short version: Ink Heroes is a free game for a general audience (13+) that shows ads from Google AdMob. We (Tiny Tap Kids Studio) do not run any servers and do not collect, store or sell your personal information ourselves. Your game progress stays on your device. Google processes some data to show ads, as described below.

1. Who we are

Ink Heroes ("the app", "the game") is made by Tiny Tap Kids Studio ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at hrr4.h.a@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your device, in the app’s local storage: your ink drops, unlocked heroes and weapons, your team, the pages you have cleared and their stars, your best Endless wave and your top 10 Endless runs (wave, splats, team and date), daily hero trials, progress toward ad-unlocked weapons and the number of levels you have played (used to space out ads). This data is never sent to us or to any server we operate. The local Endless leaderboard shows no names and never leaves your phone.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app (on Android, iPhone or iPad). Android's own device backup may include this data in your Google account backup if you have device backup turned on, and on iPhone and iPad an iCloud Backup may include it if you have iCloud Backup turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and Google or Apple.

3. No accounts

The game has no user accounts, no online leaderboards, no chat and no user-generated content. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information. The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own and has no in-app purchases.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a full-screen ad from Google AdMob after some finished pages, never in the middle of a page. It also offers optional rewarded ads, which you can choose to watch to revive once per run, to borrow a locked hero for a level, or to work toward unlocking a hero’s weapon. Ads are limited to a “PG” content rating. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID (Android), or on iPhone and iPad the identifier for vendors, plus the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in Apple's “Allow to track your activity” prompt
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is derived
  • information about the device and app, such as model, operating system version, app version, and launch and performance diagnostics
  • your interactions with ads, such as views and taps

This data is encrypted in transit. Google uses it for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device). On iPhone and iPad, you can turn tracking off for Ink Heroes or for all apps at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent or show your privacy choices before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time with the “Privacy” button at the top right of the game's home screen, which is shown where this option is required. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.

On iPhone and iPad, Ink Heroes first shows a short message explaining why it asks, then Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. The game plays exactly the same whether you tap “Allow” or “Ask App Not to Track”; if you choose not to allow tracking, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are less personalized.

6. Children

The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and your rights

We do not hold any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to retain, export or delete. Data processed by Google for ads is handled under Google's policies linked above, and you can manage it through your Google account and your device settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools, and contact us with any questions.

8. Security

All communication between the app and Google's ad and consent services uses encryption (HTTPS/TLS). The game does not transmit your data to us, or to any party other than Google.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example because a new feature needs it, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
