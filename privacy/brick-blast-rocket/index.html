Brick Blast Rocket privacy policy

App: Brick Blast Rocket (Android, package com.tinytapkids.brickblast)

Developer: Tiny Tap Kids Studio

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Short version: Brick Blast Rocket is a free game for a general audience (13+) that shows ads from Google AdMob. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. Your game progress is saved only on your device. We (Tiny Tap Kids Studio) do not collect, store or sell your personal information ourselves. Google, as our ad provider, collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

1. Who we are

Brick Blast Rocket ("the app", "the game") is made by Tiny Tap Kids Studio ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

The game saves your level progress, stars, scores, coins, rocket upgrades and rockets, trophies, Daily Challenge streak, Endless best score and settings (sound, vibration, control options) only on your device, in the app's local storage. This data is never sent to us or to any server we operate. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. Android's own device backup may include this data in your Google account backup if you have device backup turned on; that backup is controlled by you and Google.

3. No accounts, no purchases, no personal information from you

The game has no user accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no user-generated content and no in-app purchases. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free because it shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited): full-screen ads between some levels, and optional rewarded video ads you can choose to watch (for example for an extra life or double coins). To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app automatically collects and shares the following with Google:

DataWhat it isWhy
Device and other identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID and similar identifiersAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention

Google uses it according to its own privacy policy. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

5. Your choices and consent

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the first time you open the game, Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) asks whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • US state privacy laws: where Google's consent tools offer regulated US-state privacy options, they are shown in the same way.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Deleting game data: see section 2. Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; data collected by Google for ads is managed under Google's policies and your Google/device settings.

6. Internet use

The game itself works offline, including the Daily Challenge (it uses your device's date). The internet connection is used only to load ads and Google's consent message.

7. Children

Brick Blast Rocket is a general-audience game intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and you have concerns, contact us at hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com.

8. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share any data with anyone, other than the data that the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as described in section 4.

9. Security

Your game progress never leaves your device. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy (for example, if we add new features such as online leaderboards), we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing.

11. Contact
