Doodle Derby privacy policy

App: Doodle Derby (Android, package com.tinytapkids.doodlederby)

Developer: Tiny Tap Kids Studio

Website: https://tinytapkidsstudio.com

Contact: hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com

Effective date: November 27, 2026 (version 0.1.3 and later)

Short version: Doodle Derby is a free build-a-cart battle game for players aged 13 and over. It has no chat and nothing to type. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses your Play Games name as your racing name (you can pick a made-up name instead) and keeps a copy of your progress in your Google Play Games cloud save. It offers optional video ads that you can choose to watch for Glitter. Optional Glitter packs are sold through Google Play. The online Arena and weekly leaderboard share only your cart build, your racing name and your trophies. We never ask for your email or any other contact details.

1. Who we are

Doodle Derby ("the app", "the game") is made by Tiny Tap Kids Studio ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your device, in the app's local storage: your parts, carts, coins, Glitter, kits, league and Arena progress, daily gift streak and settings, and simple counters of how many videos were watched today, so it can limit them. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Android's own device backup may include this data in your Google account backup if device backup is turned on. That backup is controlled by you and Google.

3. Google Play Games

On Android the game signs in to Google Play Games automatically when you are signed in to it on your device. It uses Play Games for:

  • Your racing name: the game reads your Play Games display name and, after removing unusual characters and checking it against a list of words that are not allowed, uses it as your name in the Arena and on the weekly leaderboard. You can choose a made-up name from fixed word lists instead at any time.
  • Cloud save: a copy of your game progress (the same data as in section 2) is stored in your Google Play Games saved games, so you can continue on another device. When a cloud save and the device's save both have progress, the game asks which one to keep.
  • Achievements and a leaderboard of best Arena trophies, kept by Google Play Games.

The game does not receive your email address or your Google account details. Play Games data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; you can manage your Play Games profile, its visibility and saved games in the Google Play Games settings. You can play without Play Games: everything except the cloud save, achievements and the Google leaderboard works without it.

4. No chat or tracking of our own

The game has no chat, no free text and no user-generated pictures, and players never type anything. We never ask for an email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or other contact details. The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

5. Online Arena and weekly leaderboard

In the Arena, players race against "ghost" carts built by other players. The game sends our game server (hosted by Supabase) your cart build (which parts are where and their levels), your racing name, your trophy count, the results of your Arena fights (win, loss or draw) and a random ID that the game creates on your device so that it can replace your old ghost. This random ID is not linked to your Google account or your advertising ID. The server uses the results to keep a weekly leaderboard, where other players can see your racing name, trophies and wins. The game also downloads other players' ghosts and the leaderboard in the same way. Like any internet service, the server sees your IP address to answer the request; we do not use it to identify anyone. If the server cannot be reached, the Arena uses computer-built carts instead.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob), opt-in only

The game offers optional rewarded videos: you can choose to watch a short video for Glitter or to double the coins after a race, at most 5 a day. The game never shows banner ads or ads you did not ask for. Ads are limited to a "PG" content rating. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, information about the device and app, and your interactions with ads, to show, measure and (where you allow it) personalize ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's settings. Google handles this data under its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

7. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads can load, and follows your choice.

8. Purchases

Optional Glitter packs can be bought with real money from the shop. Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms; we do not receive your payment details. Nothing random is ever sold: sticker packs show their exact contents before you buy.

9. Age

Doodle Derby is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the online features, contact us and we will remove their Arena ghost and leaderboard entries.

10. Data retention and your rights

Arena ghosts are kept on our game server while the Arena runs and are replaced each time you upload a new one. Weekly leaderboard rows are kept for 10 weeks. Contact us if you want your ghost and leaderboard rows removed; tell us your racing name. Your cloud save, achievements and Google leaderboard scores are kept by Google Play Games; you can delete them in the Play Games settings. Everything else stays on your device. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools.

11. Security

Communication between the app and the game server, and between the app and Google's services, is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example because a new feature needs it, we will update this page and its effective date. We will also mention significant changes in the app's release notes. Versions up to 0.1.2 were made for children and families; from 0.1.3 the game is for players aged 13 and over.

13. Contact
