Doodle Derby privacy policy

App: Doodle Derby (Android, package com.tinytapkids.doodlederby)

Developer: Tiny Tap Kids Studio

Website: https://tinytapkidsstudio.com

Contact: hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Short version: Doodle Derby is a free build-a-cart game for children and families. It has no accounts, no chat and nothing to type. It offers optional video ads that a player can choose to watch for Glitter, and every ad request is marked as child-directed, so ads are not personalized. Optional Glitter packs are sold through Google Play behind a grown-up check. The online Arena shares only your cart build, a made-up cart name and your trophy count. We never ask for or collect your name, email or any other personal information.

1. Who we are

Doodle Derby ("the app", "the game") is made by Tiny Tap Kids Studio ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your device, in the app's local storage: your parts, carts, coins, Glitter, kits, league and Arena progress, daily gift streak and settings, and simple counters of how many videos were watched today, so it can limit them. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Android's own device backup may include this data in your Google account backup if device backup is turned on. That backup is controlled by you and Google.

3. No accounts, chat or tracking of our own

The game has no user accounts, no chat, no free text and no user-generated pictures. Players never type a name: Arena names are made from fixed lists of friendly words. We never ask for an email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information. The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

4. Online Arena

In the Arena, players race against "ghost" carts built by other players. When the online Arena is available, the game sends our game server (hosted by Supabase) your cart build (which parts are where and their levels), your made-up Arena name, your trophy count and a random ID that the game creates on your device so that it can replace your old ghost. This random ID is not linked to your name, your Google account or your advertising ID. The game also downloads other players' ghosts in the same way. Like any internet service, the server sees your IP address to answer the request; we do not use it to identify anyone. If the server cannot be reached, the Arena uses computer-built carts instead.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob), opt-in only

The game offers optional rewarded videos: a player can choose to watch a short video for Glitter or to double the coins after a race, at most 5 a day. The game never shows banner ads or ads the player did not ask for. Every ad request is marked as child-directed and under the age of consent, and ads are limited to a "G" content rating, so ads are not personalized and the advertising ID is not used for ads targeting. To show, measure and protect these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may still process limited data, such as your IP address, information about the device and app, and your interactions with the ad. Google handles this data under its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

6. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to show a consent message before ads can load. Requests are always marked as under the age of consent, so ads stay non-personalized whatever is chosen.

7. Purchases

Optional Glitter packs can be bought with real money. Every purchase starts with a grown-up check (a sum to answer) and is processed by Google Play under Google's terms. We do not receive your payment details. Nothing random is ever sold: sticker packs show their exact contents before you buy.

8. Children

Doodle Derby is made for children and families and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and your rights

Arena ghosts are kept on our game server while the Arena runs and are replaced each time you upload a new one. Contact us if you want your ghost removed; tell us your cart's Arena name. Everything else stays on your device. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools.

10. Security

Communication between the app and the game server, and between the app and Google's ad and consent services, is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example because a new feature needs it, we will update this page and its effective date. We will also mention significant changes in the app's release notes.
