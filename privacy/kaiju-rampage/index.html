Kaiju Rampage privacy policy

App: Kaiju Rampage (Android, package com.tinytapkids.kaijurampage)
Developer: Tiny Tap Kids Studio
Website: https://tinytapkidsstudio.com
Contact: hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com
Effective date: October 27, 2026

Short version: Kaiju Rampage is a free game for a general audience (13+) that shows ads from Google AdMob. It offers optional in-app purchases (Legendary kaiju) that are processed entirely by Google Play. You can optionally sign in with Google Play Games to back up your progress to the cloud and appear on leaderboards; without it, your progress stays only on your device. We (Tiny Tap Kids Studio) do not run any servers and do not collect, store or sell your personal information ourselves. Google, as our ad, payment and game-services provider, processes some data as described below.

1. Who we are

Kaiju Rampage ("the app", "the game") is made by Tiny Tap Kids Studio ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, upgrades, unlocked monsters and maps, Threat Levels, best scores, Legend Ascension best waves, daily missions, reward chests, a list of the in-app products you own, and settings such as sound and graphics quality) on your device, in the app's local storage. This data is never sent to us or to any server we operate (see section 7 for the optional Google Play Games cloud backup). You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Android's own device backup may include this data in your Google account backup if you have device backup turned on; that backup is controlled by you and Google.

3. No accounts of our own, no personal information from you

The game has no user accounts of its own, no chat and no user-generated content. The only sign-in is the optional Google Play Games sign-in described in section 7. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information. The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: full-screen ads between some runs, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for a reward (a revive, a reroll or double coins). Ads are limited to a "PG" content rating. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and shares with Google: your device's advertising ID and app set ID, IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), information about the device and app (for example model, operating system version, app version, launch and performance diagnostics) and your interactions with ads (views, taps). This data is encrypted in transit. Google uses it for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as described in Google's policies:
How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

5. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent or show your privacy choices before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy choices" button in the game's Settings (shown where this option is required). If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.

6. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Kaiju Rampage is free to play. Every Legendary kaiju can still be unlocked with coins earned by playing; as an option you can buy a single Legendary kaiju or all of them as one-time, non-consumable in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play: your payment details are entered in Google Play's own purchase screen and are never seen, received or stored by the game or by us. The game receives from Google Play only which of its products you own (and a purchase token used to confirm the purchase with Google Play), keeps that list on your device so the kaiju stay unlocked, and uses "Restore Purchases" in Settings to ask Google Play again. Google Play's handling of purchase data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Google Play Terms of Service. Refunds are handled through Google Play; a refunded kaiju is locked again unless you also unlocked it with coins.

7. Google Play Games (optional sign-in, cloud save, leaderboards)

If Google Play Games is available on your device, the game uses Google Play Games Services to sign you in with your Play Games profile. When you are signed in, the game stores a copy of your game progress (the same data as in section 2) in Google Play Games "Saved Games" in your Google account, so you can restore it on a new phone, and may submit your best Legend Ascension wave to Play Games leaderboards, where it is shown with your Play Games gamer name and avatar to other players according to your Play Games profile settings. The game receives your Play Games player ID and display name only to show your sign-in status; we do not receive your email address. You can sign out, change your profile visibility or delete your Play Games data and saved games in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings; if you are not signed in, nothing is uploaded and the game keeps its local save only. The cloud save also includes a random install identifier generated by the app, used only to keep coin balances correct across devices; it is not linked to your identity or used for ads. This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

8. Children

The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and your rights

We do not hold any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to retain, export or delete. Data processed by Google (ads, purchases, Play Games saved games and leaderboards) is handled under Google's policies linked above; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data; for data held by Google, use Google's tools, and contact us with any questions.

10. Security

All communication between the app and Google's ad, consent, Google Play Billing and Google Play Games services uses encryption (HTTPS/TLS). The game itself does not transmit your data to us or to any other party.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy (for example because a new feature needs it), we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

12. Contact
