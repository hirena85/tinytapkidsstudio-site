Dino Smash City privacy policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026. Developer: Tiny Tap Kids Studio. Contact: hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com

Dino Smash City ("the app") is designed for children. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.

Data stored on the device

The app saves only a few small settings on the device itself: whether the full version has been unlocked, the sound and graphics settings in the Parents menu, the last world and dino picked, the stickers earned and the dress-up choices for each dino. These never leave the device, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Unlock Everything" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. A parental question protects it inside the app. We never receive your payment details. Google handles the purchase under the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). To check whether the purchase exists, the app asks Google Play on the device.

Sounds

All sounds are generated by the app on the device. The app does not record or listen to anyone.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. If you think information was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
