Fudders NGMI privacy policy

App: Fudders NGMI (Android package and iOS bundle ID com.tinytapkids.fuddersngmi, for Android, iPhone and iPad)
Developer: Tiny Tap Kids Studio
Website: https://tinytapkidsstudio.com
Contact: hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com
Effective date: November 17, 2026

Short version: Fudders NGMI is a free game for a general audience (13+) that shows ads from Google AdMob. It has no in-app purchases and no real money of any kind. On Android you can optionally sign in with Google Play Games for cloud save, leaderboards and achievements; without it, and on iPhone and iPad, your progress stays only on your device. We (Tiny Tap Kids Studio) do not run any servers and do not collect, store or sell your personal information ourselves. Google (ads, and on Android game services) processes some data as described below.

1. Who we are

Fudders NGMI ("the app", "the game") is made by Tiny Tap Kids Studio ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, upgrades, unlocked Fudders, best scores, missions, the Total Capitulation world and settings such as sound and graphics quality) on your device, in the app's local storage. This data is never sent to us or to any server we operate (see section 6 for the optional Google Play Games cloud backup on Android). You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app (on Android, iPhone or iPad). Android's own device backup may include this data in your Google account backup if you have device backup turned on, and on iPhone and iPad an iCloud Backup may include it if you have iCloud Backup turned on; those backups are controlled by you and Google or Apple.

3. No accounts of our own, no personal information from you

The game has no user accounts of its own, no chat and no user-generated content. The only sign-in is the optional Google Play Games sign-in on Android described in section 6; the iPhone and iPad version has no sign-in at all. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information. The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own. It works offline.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: full-screen ads between some runs, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for a reward. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and shares with Google: your device's advertising ID and app set ID (Android), or on iPhone and iPad the identifier for vendors plus the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt (see section 5), IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), information about the device and app (for example model, operating system version, app version, launch and performance diagnostics) and your interactions with ads (views, taps). This data is encrypted in transit. Google uses it for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as described in Google's policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device). On iPhone and iPad you can turn tracking off for Fudders NGMI or for all apps at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform and Apple App Tracking Transparency)

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent or show your privacy choices before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy choices" button in the game's Settings (shown where this option is required). If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.

On iPhone and iPad, the game first shows a short message explaining why it asks, then Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. The game plays exactly the same whether you tap "Allow" or "Ask App Not to Track"; if you do not allow tracking, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are less personalized.

6. Google Play Games (Android only: optional sign-in, cloud save, leaderboards, achievements)

The iPhone and iPad version does not use Google Play Games or Apple Game Center. On Android, if Google Play Games is available on your device, the game can sign you in with your Play Games profile. When you are signed in, the game stores a copy of your progress (the same data as in section 2) in Google Play Games "Saved Games" in your Google account, and may submit your scores to Play Games leaderboards and unlock achievements, shown with your Play Games gamer name and avatar according to your profile settings. The game receives your Play Games player ID and display name only to show your sign-in status; we do not receive your email address. You can sign out, change your profile visibility or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings. This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

7. No purchases, no real money

Fudders NGMI has no in-app purchases. The coins and other currencies in the game are earned by playing, have no real-world value, cannot be bought, sold or exchanged, and are not connected to any real cryptocurrency, token or NFT. The game's crypto jokes are parody.

8. Children

The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and your rights

We do not hold any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to retain, export or delete. Data processed by Google (ads, Play Games saved games, leaderboards and achievements) is handled under Google's policies linked above; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data; for data held by Google, use Google's tools, and contact us with any questions.

10. Security

All communication between the app and Google's ad, consent and Google Play Games services uses encryption (HTTPS/TLS). The game itself does not transmit your data to us or to any other party.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy (for example because a new feature needs it), we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

12. Contact

Tiny Tap Kids Studio: hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com
