Christmas Town Fun privacy policy

App: Christmas Town Fun (Android, package com.tinytapkids.christmastownfun)

Developer: Tiny Tap Kids Studio

Website: https://tinytapkidsstudio.com

Contact: hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Short version: Christmas Town Fun is a free collection of holiday games made for children. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, no chat and no tracking. We (Tiny Tap Kids Studio) do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information from anyone, including children. Everything the app saves stays on the device. The only thing that uses the internet is an optional tip for grown-ups, which is handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Who we are

Christmas Town Fun ("the app") is made by Tiny Tap Kids Studio ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com.

2. Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings, contacts, location, device identifiers or advertising IDs. It does not use the camera or microphone. It contains no third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no links that take children out of the app.

3. Information that stays on the device

To remember progress, the app saves a small file in its own private storage on the device. It contains things like the stars earned in each game, which Advent Calendar doors have been opened, the sound volume setting, and creations kept in some games (for example a decorated tree, snowflakes, coloring pages, the Fish Book and picture-only letters to Santa). The letter to Santa is made only from pictures and stickers; there is no typing and no names. This file is never sent to us or to any server. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include it in your own Google account backup, which is controlled by you and Google.

4. Optional tip for grown-ups (Google Play Billing)

All games are free and nothing is locked. In the "Grown-ups" area, which is protected by a parent check that young children cannot complete, a parent can choose to leave an optional one-time tip to support the developers. The tip does not unlock any content. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play using your Google account; we never see or receive payment card details, names or email addresses. Google Play may share an anonymous order record with us (such as the order number, product and date) so we can confirm the purchase, as described in Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Payments Privacy Notice

The app's internet permission is used only by Google Play for this optional tip. All games work without an internet connection.

5. Children's privacy

The app is designed for children ages 3 to 8 and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not share or sell any information, because we do not collect any.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. If a future version of the app changes what it does with information, we will update this policy before releasing that version.

8. Contact us

Questions or requests: hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com
