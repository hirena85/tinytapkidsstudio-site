Kids Fun Games privacy policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026. Developer: Tiny Tap Kids Studio. Contact: hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com

Kids Fun Games ("the app") is designed for young children. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.

Data stored on the device

The app saves only a few small things on the device itself: whether the full version has been unlocked, the sound, voice and graphics choices in the Parents menu (including the High/Low graphics choice for the 3D games), Toy Trains track layouts, worlds, puzzle progress, stars and train choices, UFO Pickup stars, ship and pilot choices, the things on the home planet and the Sticker Book (Beam Buddies 3D uses the same ship and adds its stars and beamed-up things there), Beam Buddies 3D mission progress, the last world and attachment used, and its graphics choice, Dig & Build stars, treasures found and the last mode played, and which Spray & Shine things have been washed clean. These never leave the device, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Unlock All Games" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. A parental question protects it inside the app. We never receive your payment details. Google handles the purchase under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). To check whether the purchase exists, the app asks Google Play on the device.

Speech

Spoken words come from the text-to-speech engine installed on the device. The app does not record or listen to anyone.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. If you think information was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
