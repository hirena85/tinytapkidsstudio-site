Privacy at Tiny Tap Kids Studio

Effective date: November 14, 2026 · Contact: hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com

We make games, not profiles. Most of our games keep everything they save on your device, and our games for children collect no personal information at all. Each app has its own privacy policy below, because each one does slightly different things (for example, some general-audience games show ads from Google AdMob).

Our games for children

Kids Fun Games, Dino Smash City and Christmas Town Fun are made for young children and follow the Google Play Families Policy. They have no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking, and they do not collect personal information from anyone. Optional purchases or tips are processed by Google Play and sit behind a parent check that young children cannot complete.

This website

This website (tinytapkidsstudio.com) is a simple information page. It does not use cookies, analytics, ads, tracking pixels, embedded videos or social media widgets, and it has no forms or accounts. Like every website, the hosting provider that serves these pages may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and to keep the site running. We do not use those logs to identify visitors.

The "Get it on Google Play" buttons are ordinary links. When you follow one, you leave this site and Google's own privacy policy applies.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your questions and requests

If you have a question about any of our games or want us to delete an email you sent us, write to hello@tinytapkidsstudio.com.

Changes

If this page or an app's policy changes, we will update it here and change its effective date.
