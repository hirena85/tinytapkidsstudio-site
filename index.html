About us

A tiny studio with a big toy box

Tiny Tap Kids Studio is a small independent studio. We build every game ourselves: the art, the sounds and the code. Some games are for little hands, others are for older players, and every one of them is tested on real phones before it ships.

🧸

Made for little hands

Big buttons, no reading needed and nothing punishing. In our kids' games you can't really lose.

🛡️

Parents first

Our kids' games have no ads and no tracking. Purchases and tips sit behind a grown-up check.

🎮

Real games for older players

Monsters, brick breakers and deep-sea dives for teens and grown-ups, free to play with fair ads.
